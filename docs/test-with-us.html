Help test the Toolbox

Try a small real task, then tell Cowboy what happened.

  1. Choose a harmless task: merge PDFs, find a résumé example, sketch a diagram, find a model’s manual or compare image tools.
  2. Start from search or a starter pack. Note confusing wording, changed prices and account requirements.
  3. Inspect the final result. Opening a tool is different from finishing.
  4. Share the task, device/browser, result and obstacle. Use fictional or public sample content.

Beginners, teachers, tradespeople and people using accessibility features are welcome. Participation is voluntary. No payment, prize or reward is promised. Board posting requires verification and owner approval. Do not post passwords, private files or other people’s personal information.
