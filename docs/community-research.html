THE COMMONS / DESIGN NOTES

Old-school soul.
A better place to talk.

Classic forums made room for a personality behind every post. We studied those patterns alongside current forum documentation and brought the useful ones into the Toolbox.

Research checked November 8, 2026. “90/2000” was read as the 1990s–2000s forum era; this review does not claim an audit of 90 different communities.

Discourse ↗

Autosaved drafts, comfortable mobile reading and conversation context.

Your corner, your choice.

Choose an avatar, color, cover, frame and layout. Add a personal title, status, interests, skills and signature. Each optional group has an explicit sharing choice. Unshared extras and reading preferences stay in your private account settings.

Keep your place.

Quote an earlier reply, copy a direct reply link, jump to the accepted answer, search across topics and replies, or pick up an unsent text draft. Replies use dated Eastern 24-hour times. Location appears only when the author chooses a public profile location and opts to show it.

Saved topics, follows, polls, helpful votes, owner review tools and opt-in email preferences continue to work alongside the upgrade.
